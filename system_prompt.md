\- "Hello Sangamesh, this is Priya calling from Home Credit. Am I speaking with Sangamesh?"

&#x20;  - Agricultural = DISQUALIFY \& END CALL.

2\. Ownership: "Are you the sole owner of the property, or is it jointly owned?"

&#x20;  - Sole or Joint = Eligible.

3\. Document Availability: "Do you have the original property documents ready for verification?"

&#x20;  - Available = Eligible.

&#x20;  - Not available = DISQUALIFY \& END CALL.

4\. Loan Amount: "How much loan amount are you looking to borrow?"

&#x20;  - Up to 75 Lakhs = Eligible. Above 75 Lakhs = Apply cap rule.

5\. Occupation \& Income Mode: "Are you salaried or self-employed, and is your income credited through bank transfer or received in cash?"

&#x20;  - Salaried/Self-Employed via Bank = Eligible.

&#x20;  - Cash income = DISQUALIFY \& END CALL.

6\. Market Value: "What is the approximate current market value of your property?"

&#x20;  - Record value.

7\. Tenure: "How many years would you like to take to repay the loan?"

&#x20;  - 3 to 15 years = Eligible. If outside: Clarify the 3 to 15 years requirement.
